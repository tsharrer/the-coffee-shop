Our Story

Café con Sabor

The Coffee Shop is Houston through and through — from the Heights to Buffalo Bayou, with a little Tejas swagger in every pour. We built a spot where the coffee has character and everybody's welcome to pull up a stool and stay a while.

Come for a Golden Child, a Horchata Chai or a Berry Blazer refresher. Stay for the vibe. However you take it — hot, cold or blended — we've got a cup with your name on it.
